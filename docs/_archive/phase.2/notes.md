# Reqirements 
Immigration Lawyers in Spain need help in filing immigration forms for their client. Lawyers generally have an assistant who does most of the paperwork. And with guidance and review by the lawyer, the applications or forms are filed. Modern tech, including AI (LLMs etc) can help in providing a solution which is modern, streamlined, automated, fresh and futuristic.

This application does not replace existing apps used by the lawyers (e.g., mycases). It is expected to integrate with them.

The Clear Path (Trust & Professionalism) direction leans into the authority and trust required in legal tech, but modernizes it. Use deep navy blues, crisp whites, and subtle silver accents. Typography would feature an authoritative serif font for headings paired with a highly legible sans-serif for dense form data. The layout would be spacious and structured, focusing on clear status indicators for application progress.
